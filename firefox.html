A history of Firefox

The project which became Firefox started as an experimental branch of the Mozilla Suite called m/b, or mozilla/browser. After it had been sufficiently developed, binaries for public testing appeared in September 2002 under the name Phoenix.

The browser was later renamed because of trademark issues. Phoenix became Firebird, and eventually the browser was renamed Firefox. Firefox became popular as an alternative to other major browsers because of its speed, customization options, support for web standards, and large collection of extensions.
