A history of Chrome

Google's Eric Schmidt opposed the development of an independent web browser for six years. He stated that at the time Google was a small company, and he did not want to go through bruising browser wars. Eventually, Google founders Sergey Brin and Larry Page hired several Mozilla Firefox developers and built a demonstration of Chrome.

Google Chrome was first released in 2008. Chrome was designed around speed, simplicity, and security. It eventually became available on multiple operating systems and developed into one of the most widely used web browsers.
